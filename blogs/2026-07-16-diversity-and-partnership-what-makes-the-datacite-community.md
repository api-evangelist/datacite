DataCite is a home for research organizations from over 70 countries, working together to connect research and advance knowledge through community-driven open infrastructure. Our membership reflects the wide range and diversity of research communities we work with, bringing together universities, national libraries, research and education networks (NRENs), research funders, museums, government bodies, archives, learned societies, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), international organizations, and others from around the world.
