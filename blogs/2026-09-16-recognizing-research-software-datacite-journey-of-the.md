Becoming a DataCite member organization is not a single milestone; it is a journey shaped by different challenges, opportunities, and lessons learned. In the DataCite Member Stories blog series, we share their experiences as they work to connect research, improve discovery, and support open science using DataCite infrastructure. In this post, we feature the Helmholtz-Zentrum Dresden-Rossendorf (HZDR) from Germany, which shares its DataCite journey, the impact of adopting DataCite DOIs, and what it means to be part of the global DataCite community.
